Support

Answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Contact us below.

I can't hear the purr.
  • Turn the volume up with your phone's side buttons while Cuddles is purring.
  • The Ring/Silent switch doesn't matter – Cuddles plays even in silent mode.
  • If you're connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, the purr plays there.
  • If music or a podcast from another app starts, it can stop the purr. Switch on "Play alongside other audio" in the app's Settings to hear both.
Why does the vibration stop when I lock my phone?

iOS only lets apps vibrate while they're open on screen. The sound keeps purring with the screen locked, but the vibration needs Cuddles open. For long sessions on your chest or lap, use Lap Mode: it dims the screen and keeps both going. "Keep screen awake while purring" in Settings helps too. On devices without rich haptics, the purr is sound-only.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Support tab and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my Cuddles Club subscription?

In iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cuddles. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period – you keep the Club until the end of the period you've paid for. Gifts for the creator's cat are one-time tips: they never renew and there's nothing to cancel.

My cuddle reminders don't arrive.
  • Make sure notifications are allowed for Cuddles in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Cuddles.
  • Check that "Cuddle reminders" is on in the app's Settings › Reminders.
  • A Focus mode (like Sleep or Do Not Disturb) can silence them.
  • Cuddles schedules reminders about two weeks ahead each time you open it, so open it now and then.
How do I change my cat or its name?

On the Purr screen, open Settings (the sliders button) › Your cat. Pick a different cat or type a new name – your stats and bond stay as they are.
