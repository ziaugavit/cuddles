Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Cuddles ("the app") plays a purring cat you can hear and feel, to help you relax and sleep. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there's no account, your cat, stats and reminders stay on your phone, we don't show ads, we don't track you across apps, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Cuddles is published by Vitalijus Žiauga, Lithuania ("we", "us"), the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at any time through the contact link.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your cat and its name, and your settings (purr, background sound, sleep timer, vibration strength and other preferences).
  • Purr statistics: minutes purred, streaks and your bond with your cat.
  • Reminders. The cuddle reminders you set are scheduled as local notifications by iOS on your device. No server is involved.
  • Widget data. Your cat, its name and your stats are shared with Cuddles' own widgets through an App Group on your device. They don't leave your phone.
  • Location – only on the Adopt tab. When you open Adopt and allow location access, the app uses your approximate location on your device to search Apple Maps for cat shelters and rescues nearby. Your location is not sent to us and is not stored by the app. The map search itself is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Everything else in Cuddles works without location access.

Data we process

Usage analytics: screens viewed, features used (purr sessions and their duration, purr and background-sound choices, sleep timer, reminders on or off, the cat breed you picked), settings changed, onboarding and purchase steps, device model, iOS and app version, language, approximate country/region (derived from your IP address), and a random app-instance identifier. To unlock the Cuddles Club and restore your purchases. Apple (payment) and RevenueCat Inc. (purchase management). We never see your payment details.
Support messages: your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. To answer you and fix problems. Us (via email, Google)

We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), advertising consent is switched off in our analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not share or sell data to advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics: Settings › Privacy › "Share anonymous usage data" in the app.
  • Reminders: switch off "Cuddle reminders" in Settings › Reminders in the app, or turn off notifications in iOS Settings › Cuddles.
  • Location: Cuddles only asks when you open the Adopt tab. You can change or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings › Cuddles › Location.
  • Data on your phone: deleting the app deletes your cat, settings, statistics and reminders.

Legal basis (EU/UK users)

We process purchase data to provide the subscription or gift you bought (contract), analytics on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app – which you can object to at any time with the switch above – and support messages to answer your request. Location is used on your device only with your permission (consent), which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings.

How long we keep data

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for your subscription and as required by tax law. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to restrict or object to its processing. To make a request, use the contact link. Because analytics data isn't linked to your identity, include your app-instance details or the email you contacted us with so we can find it. Data that stays on your phone never reaches us – you control it, and deleting the app removes it. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority – in Lithuania, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Children

Cuddles is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us.
